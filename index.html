Caya Chocolate is taking a break!

We welcomed a new adventure in April, and we're taking a break from our existing adventure to focus on this new one 😊

Caya's single-origin chocolate bars are crafted to showcase the unique and complex flavors of each cocoa bean. We use only minimal, high-quality ingredients - cocoa beans, sugar, cocoa butter, and milk powder for milk chocolate - and add no preservatives.

Caya was lovingly founded by passionate craft chocolate makers Pradeep and Deeksha. We are dedicated to sharing our love of chocolates with casual and discerning palates alike.
