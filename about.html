Our Story

Caya was lovingly founded by passionate craft chocolate makers Pradeep and Deeksha. We are dedicated to sharing our love of chocolates with casual and discerning palates alike.

Making great chocolate is about the process as much as it is about the bean. Proudly made with love in our California home kitchen, our chocolate is carefully crafted to deliver a delicious and unforgettable tasting experience.

Our Philosophy

Single-Origin Excellence

Our chocolate bars are crafted to showcase the unique and complex flavors of each cocoa bean from different regions around the world.

Minimal Ingredients

We use only minimal, high-quality ingredients - cocoa beans, sugar, cocoa butter, and milk powder for milk chocolate - and add no preservatives.

Artisan Craftsmanship

Every bar is carefully handcrafted in our California home kitchen with attention to detail and passion for the craft.
